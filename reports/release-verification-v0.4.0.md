## Release Boundary

Ready for bounded public technical scrutiny of the protocol and owned-use
evidence. Not a production-qualified universal gateway, endorsed standard,
independent human acceptance study or automatically venue-ready submission.
Current-content recovery is conditional, not cross-provider exactly-once or
atomic remote revocation. Full provider descendants, hosted-MCP authentication,
sustained multi-host operation and external cohorts remain outside this result.

Only selected already-public source histories, synthetic/public observations,
original manuscript and checksums are included. No Studio/Hook changes, new
Azure resource, customer/protected research data, invitation or formal
manuscript submission is part of this release.
