# External Evaluation, Sealed Inputs, and Outcome Custody

Editorial date: 18 October 2026, America/Toronto (EDT, UTC-04:00).
Working procedure, not an executed agreement or an appointment of a custodian.
The completed bounded runtime study has zero external evaluations. Implemented
[seal/run/score interfaces](external-evaluation-cli.md) support the process but
do not create independently controlled custody or external authorship.
Freeze/access/run events use UTC. Any later publication date is separate.

This procedure supplements [PROTOCOL.md](../../PROTOCOL.md) and the
[collaboration contract](../../collaboration-contract.md); it does not relax
them. No external cohort, independent review result, or laboratory participation
has been established by writing this document. Unrelated protected collaboration
data is excluded and must not be sought, copied, or relabeled for this project.

## 1. Choose the Correct Track

| Track | Suitable material | Permitted description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public development | Synthetic, rights-cleared counterexamples and exposed regressions | Development evidence, even when externally submitted |
| Same-project frozen pilot | Inputs and literal expectations frozen before execution/results, with disclosed shared project control | Internal or same-project evaluation; not independent institutional confirmation |
| Protected external cohort | Independently authored native inputs and expected outcomes, effective custody, controlled execution and independent disputed-score governance | Independent only to the extent the actual role/access/control evidence establishes |

For the completed pilot, implementation proceeded in parallel from the public
contract; the declared process withheld case JSON until freeze. This was a
pre-execution/pre-result freeze, not a pre-implementation freeze. Subsequent
regressions are exposed development material, not an independent protected
cohort. A future external evaluation must record actual access and control.

Official upstream schemas, including the selected GitHub OpenAPI commit
`755ee33b4a81d51f6f07a902cce26cbec3b822f2`, are interface references. They can
constrain labels but are not independent governance labels, permission grants,
or evidence of completed tasks. Preserve the upstream MIT notice on adopted
extracts, and record their exact source path and extraction digest.

## 2. Propose Scope Without Disclosing Cases

Use a public issue only for a non-sensitive proposal: domain, intended test
question, relevant capabilities, approximate practical evaluation constraints,
rights basis, and whether protected custody is requested. Do not put active
hidden input, expected outcomes, case filenames that reveal the task, private
traces, credentials, or customer content into an issue or pull request.

Before accepting a protected transfer, agree on actual custodians, access
channels, roles, resource authorization, retention, scoring, publication freedom,
rights, and disclosure rules. This document is not a secure upload service.
If those arrangements do not exist, stop intake rather than ask someone to send
hidden material to the candidate developers.

Public development submissions need a minimal synthetic input, supported
profile/clause, independently explained expected mapping and decision, actual
output if already observed, versions, exposure history, and contributor authority
under [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Distinguish a reference rationale
from an observed provider event. Unsolicited material is not automatically
licensed, and later terms do not create retroactive assent.

## 3. Independent Roles and Conflicts

Record actual people/organizations, appointment and removal control, funding,
contractual vetoes, access rights, and conflicts for each role before the run.
No role is filled or endorsement implied by this table.

| Role | Responsibility | Independence restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Case authors | Native inputs, environment facts, intended goals and challenge families | Do not derive protected cases from candidate output or tune the scored implementation |
| Reference authors/labelers | Coupled reference effects, unknowns, expected processing and policy outcomes | Cannot be developers of a scored implementation whose protected answers they author |
| Adjudicators | Resolve factual/label disagreements with preserved rationale | Recuse from disputes concerning their own implementation or labels where conflict prevents independent resolution |
| Input custodian | Seal and release inputs to the controlled runner only | No active protected developer preview; log every grant and access |
| Outcome custodian/scorer | Seal expected outcomes and apply frozen scoring | Developers cannot access active answers, decide their disputed scores, or control an appeal |
| Runner | Execute fixed artifacts within authorized budgets | Receives only the inputs and operational facts needed for execution; no tuning against labels |
| Independent appeal panel | Resolve disputes and authorize amendments under frozen rules | Maintain a real independent quorum after recusals; no candidate-controlled deciding veto |

Where one party necessarily holds multiple roles, document the overlap. Two
agents, languages, repositories, contractors, or organizations with common
deciding control are not automatically independent. Funding disclosure alone
does not remove control over unfavorable outcomes. If an independent quorum is
unavailable, defer adjudication or label the result internal; do not waive the
restriction and still call the result independent.

## 4. Author References Before Candidate Exposure

Construct native inputs and references using the public contract, authorized
environment facts, and a written labeling guide. Keep operation/resource/
destination/data/quantity relationships intact. Specify may versus observed,
direct versus descendant scope, known versus none versus unknown, binding and
evidence requirements, expected processing result, current-authority decision,
and the original useful-task success predicate.

Use at least two qualified labelers for the independently assessed subsets
declared by the protocol. Preserve disagreements, adjudication, uncertainty,
and cases lacking a defensible reference. Agreement statistics are not truth.
Do not use either the proposed mapper or a baseline's own evaluator as the
defining independent oracle. A policy engine that defines expected answers is
a noncompetitive oracle diagnostic unless separate reference adjudication is
established.

For pure authorization, use independently specified reference effects, not
producer output. For integrated admission, score the same original intent and
authority expectation against candidate processing and actual gate behavior.
Observation evidence must have an explicit universe and time window. In the
GitHub profile, direct comment readback cannot establish that notifications or
webhooks were absent.

## 5. Seal Inputs and Expected Outcomes Separately

Keep native inputs/context and expected outcomes/reference rationales in separate
access-controlled, encrypted packages. They may share a cohort manifest but
need separate permissions and key handling. The runner receives a needed input;
that does not entitle it or developers to the expected answer. Raw traces may
reveal the input and therefore need the same protection.

Create an authenticated manifest identifying the packages, schema/profile
versions, creation/freeze timestamps, content digests, authors/custodians,
encryption/key custody arrangements, access policy, and retirement conditions.
Keep access events and approved transfers in an append-only or otherwise
tamper-evident record with its actual trust assumptions disclosed. Signatures
and timestamps establish particular commitments, not semantic correctness or
proof that an author never encountered related material.

A plaintext hash is identity, not secrecy; small or predictable hidden cases
can be guessed against it. Use confidential manifests or properly randomized
commitments where appropriate, with salts/nonces retained by custodians. Do not
publish sensitive package paths, arguments, exact expected fields, or examples
that make a protected family recoverable.

Document hosting, model/provider logging, retention, human operator access,
network egress, telemetry, caches, and error reports. The system under test
necessarily sees its task; developers must not receive that active material
before or during protected evaluation. Unknown hosted-provider access is an
exposure limitation, not verified sealed execution. Default to synthetic inputs
and an independently controlled offline runner where feasible.

## 6. Freeze Before Execution and Results

Before the protected system sees active inputs, jointly freeze:

1. The specification, effective profiles, native contracts, upstream references,
   input/reference packages, and their byte identities.
2. Candidate and complete baseline sources, dependencies, policy, trusted
   principal/context acquisition, engine/wrapper semantics, and enforcement.
3. Track eligibility, base-intent/family structure, original success checks,
   scope/observation windows, semantic equality, ambiguity, and exclusions.
4. Mapping/decision/utility/unknown/failure endpoints, scheduled denominators,
   pairing/weighting, analysis plan, and any justified non-inferiority rule.
5. Resource and submission budgets, request/rate/cost limits, randomization,
   order, retries, invalid-run rules, safety stops, and readback/recovery.
6. Role/access agreements, conflicts, appeal/quorum rules, release/retirement,
   rights, and independent publication of unfavorable findings.

Freeze actual implementation artifacts before the run; do not falsely claim
they were frozen before their development began. Complete public-development
competence checks and equivalent tuning budgets for every arm first. A
complete structured OPA adapter, and any actually integrated Cedar arm, must
receive equivalent facts and protection. Hints-only or deliberately lossy
mapping is an ablation, not the primary competitor.

No developer preview of active protected inputs/answers is permitted merely
because the manifest is frozen. Limit submissions and feedback granularity to
prevent adaptive extraction. New code, labels, policy, or scoring after a
failure requires a versioned amendment/new run. The old attempt remains in the
ledger and the learned family is exposed development for subsequent versions.

## 7. Failure, Stop, and Rerun Rules

Every scheduled case and attempt remains recorded, including one that never
started. Preassign a single primary status plus orthogonal failure/observation
fields where needed so counts reconcile without erasing simultaneous faults.

| Event | Required accounting and action |
| --- | --- |
| Unsupported profile/operation or missing trusted evidence | Withhold affected execution; retain unsupported/untrusted classification; no correct-block credit |
| Invalid input/configuration/output | Retain exact failing stage and frozen contract expectation; do not recast as semantic uncertainty |
| Mapper/consumer/PDP crash, timeout, limit, or missing output | Failure and non-completion; not a correct review/deny merely because no request was sent |
| Independently established applicable denial plus unrelated unknown | Retain the denial and the unknown separately; do not widen authority |
| Provider rejection or observer failure | Record attempted delivery, actual status, and what remains unknown; no fake successful readback |
| Delivery may have begun | Retain reservation; reconcile read-only under budget; never automatically repeat create to obtain a result |
| Source/lock/policy drift or environment failure | Mark affected attempts under frozen invalid-run rules; preserve them and explain rerun eligibility |
| Confirmed forbidden effect or exhausted safety budget | Stop affected live execution, preserve evidence, and follow the agreed incident/recovery procedure |
| Hidden-input/label disclosure | Quarantine affected family, log access and exposure, mark affected versions; no silent replacement with easier cases |
| Oracle or scorer defect | Retain original outcomes, independent adjudication and correction record; freeze revised analysis and identify exploratory results |

Reruns get new attempt IDs linked to the original. A permitted infrastructure
rerun is not a fresh independent sample. Publish attempt-level failures and the
predeclared primary attempt/intent scoring rule; no selecting the best run.
Cases without defensible references remain visible as ambiguous under the
frozen rule. No post hoc deletion to make accuracy or safety look better.

## 8. Scoring and Release

Separate mapping correctness, authorization on reference effects, integrated
admission, actual provider effects, useful task completion, and total cost.
Retain unknowns and failures in the main scheduled populations. Report correct
and unnecessary reviews, legitimate work withheld for any reason, missing
results, and per-stage failure counts. An all-block system cannot win by a
single false-allow statistic. Joint verified useful-and-safe completion needs
adequate observation of the declared universe; unknown outcomes do not qualify.

Use the [measurement contract](../../paper/measurement-contract.md) to reconcile
counts, versions, raw timings, all attempts, baselines, and scoped observations.
Variants/repeats remain clustered by base intent. A small convenience cohort
does not establish population coverage or production tail latency.

Keep active protected inputs, labels, raw traces, failures, and replay artifacts
sealed for authorized independent auditors. Public reporting uses reviewed
aggregates/redactions and states what auditors actually accessed. Neither a
helpful diagnostic example nor a raw failure traceback is automatically safe
to publish. After cohort retirement, release only rights-cleared, minimized
material under the agreed terms with exposure history and original failures.

MIT approval for the repositories covers their selected original work, not
automatic rights to every contributed cohort or third-party publication. No
patent clearance, standards-body endorsement, legal certification, or external
authorship is created by participation. This procedure is not an invitation,
an appointment, or a claim of an executed protected evaluation.
