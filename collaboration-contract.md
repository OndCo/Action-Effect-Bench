# Proposed Collaboration and Custody Contract

This is a role/design proposal, not an executed agreement or assignment of work.

## Independent Roles

- Spec editors settle public semantics and maintain change history.
- Case authors define native inputs and references before candidate inspection.
- Annotators/adjudicators record facts, uncertainties and disagreements.
- Custodians seal protected inputs/labels and log access.
- Candidate developers receive development material, not active protected
  inputs/labels before or during a confirmatory run. Freeze does not waive this.
- Runners execute frozen implementations within the agreed budget.
- Scorers apply frozen rules, retaining failures and all planned denominators.

One organization may cover several roles when necessary, but the exposure and
conflict must be disclosed. "Third party" is not synonymous with "independent"
if the party received candidate labels or helped tune the tested implementation.

For an independently labeled track, the restrictions in PROTOCOL sections 4-5
are mandatory: no self-authored protected answers, self-scoring of disputes or
controlling an appeal for one's own scored implementation. Document actual
control, funding, appointments and independent quorum after recusal; when absent,
defer or label the decision internal. Protected raw results remain sealed for
auditors until cohort retirement. Test-system exposure and hosted-provider access
are documented separately from authorized developer access.

## Separate Research Collaborations

Related work on vendor-neutral skill-boundary semantics, threat models and
independent evaluation remains separately scoped. No duty to maintain a registry,
endorse this proposal, supply hidden cases or act as a certification authority
is inferred from another collaboration.

Participation here requires explicit agreement on scope, authorship, publication
freedom, case custody, access and IP. Existing reserved families and runtime
mappings are not accessed, relabelled or published by this draft. A skill contract
need not use this registry's identifiers or OSuite code.

## External Contribution Paths

An implementer may build a consumer from the public spec without OSuite. A tool
author may contribute declared mappings with clearly labeled provenance. A lab
may contribute a separately sealed evaluation cohort. An operator may contribute
authorized, minimized failure reports. None is required to purchase a product.

Before soliciting, accepting, merging, redistributing or substantively adopting
outside contributions, make the approved license/IP and provenance mechanism
effective and record authority/assent or a sufficient existing grant. This covers
text, figures, mappings, case inputs and labels, not just code; unsolicited
material is not automatically licensed and later terms are not retroactive.
For this public draft, the current intake requirements are in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) under the repository's [MIT license](LICENSE).
Before protected evaluation, record named roles, access controls, conflicts and
freeze signatures. No outside role is filled merely by writing it into this file.
